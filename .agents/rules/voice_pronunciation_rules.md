# Rule: Voice Script Pronunciation & Language Standards (DT & IDT)

## Purpose & Scope
This rule enforces strict uniformity and crystal-clear pronunciation across all audio narration scripts (`voice_sync_text`, `malayalam_audio_script`, `malayalam_narration`) generated by AI models and speech synthesis pipelines in this repository.

---

## Absolute Core Directives

### 1. Sections, Sub-sections, Clauses & Rules Must Be in English
- **Always write and speak section numbers in standard English Latin script.**
- **Correct**: `Section 49(1)`, `Section 2(42A)`, `Section 48`, `Section 55(2)(aa)`, `Section 112A`, `Section 115BAC`, `Rule 8`, `Explanation 1`.
- **Strictly Prohibited**: Never translate or phonetically spell section numbers in Malayalam script (e.g. NEVER write `സെക്ഷൻ നാൽപ്പത്തിയൊമ്പത്`, `സെക്ഷൻ രണ്ട് സബ്സെക്ഷൻ നാൽപ്പത്തിരണ്ട് എ`).

### 2. Dates, Years & Assessment Periods Must Be in English
- **Always write dates, financial years, and assessment years in English Latin script.**
- **Correct**: `1st April 2001`, `31st March 2024`, `1-4-2001`, `Financial Year 2023-24`, `Assessment Year 2024-25`, `Previous Year 2023-24`.
- **Strictly Prohibited**: Never translate dates, months, or years into Malayalam words or Malayalam numerals (e.g. NEVER write `ഒന്നാം തിയ്യതി ഏപ്രിൽ മാസം രണ്ടായിരത്തിയൊന്ന്` or `വർഷം 2001-ൽ`).

### 3. Universal Domain Principle: ALL Tax, Statutory, Legal & Financial Terminology Must Be in English
- **The Core Rule**: Do NOT limit this to a fixed list of terms. **ANY and EVERY word or phrase that belongs to the domain of law, taxation, finance, accounting, procedures, or computation MUST remain in standard English.**
- In Kerala CA lectures, students study the statutory law and write exams exclusively in English. Artificial translations or Malayalam neologisms create confusion and sound unnatural.
- **Scope of Universal English Terms includes, but is NOT limited to**:
  - **Heads & Concepts**: `Capital Gains`, `PGBP / Profits and Gains from Business or Profession`, `Salaries`, `Income from House Property`, `Income from Other Sources`, `Agricultural Income`, `Total Income`, `Gross Total Income`, `Slump Sale`, `Buyback`, `Deemed Dividend`, `Virtual Digital Assets`, `Amalgamation`, `Demerger`.
  - **Valuation & Costs**: `Cost of Acquisition`, `Cost of Improvement`, `Indexed Cost`, `Fair Market Value`, `Stamp Duty Value`, `Full Value of Consideration`, `Actual Cost`, `Written Down Value (WDV)`, `Block of Assets`, `Depreciation`, `Arm's Length Price`.
  - **Statutory Mechanics**: `Exemption`, `Deduction`, `Rebate`, `Surcharge`, `Cess`, `Set-off`, `Carry Forward`, `Intra-head`, `Inter-head`, `Clubbing of Income`, `Disallowance`, `Presumptive Taxation`.
  - **Procedures & Entities**: `Assessee`, `Previous Owner`, `Transfer`, `Assessment Year`, `Financial Year`, `Previous Year`, `TDS`, `TCS`, `Advance Tax`, `Tax Audit`, `Return of Income`, `Faceless Assessment`, `Reassessment`, `Search & Seizure`, `Appeals`, `Rectification`, `Revision`, `Penalty`.
  - **Statutory Instruments**: `Section`, `Sub-section`, `Clause`, `Sub-clause`, `Proviso`, `Explanation`, `Notification`, `Circular`, `CBDT`, `Finance Act`.
  - **Rates & Thresholds**: Percentages and limits (`20%`, `15%`, `10%`, `4% cess`, `50 Lakhs`, `1 Crore`, `10,000`).
- **Malayalam's Specific Role (Conversational Framing Only)**:
  - Malayalam is used **strictly for conversational scaffolding, teacher explanations, and logical connectives** (e.g. `ഇവിടെ നമ്മൾ മനസ്സിലാക്കേണ്ടത്`, `ഇതിന്റെ legal rule എന്താണെന്ന് നോക്കാം`, `ഇത് calculate ചെയ്യുമ്പോൾ ശ്രദ്ധിക്കുക`, `ഇതിൽ രണ്ട് conditions satisfy ചെയ്യണം`).
  - The moment you refer to any legal concept, rule, asset, threshold, entity, action, or formula, switch instantly to English.


### 4. Spoken Malayalam Sentence Structure (Natural Code-Switching)
- The explanatory sentences, conversational connectives, logic flow, and pedagogical intuition must be in natural, friendly, spoken Malayalam.
- The technical statutory nouns, numbers, formulas, dates, and sections are seamlessly embedded in English.
- **Example of Correct Voice Script**:
  > "Section 49(1) പ്രകാരം, Previous Owner-ൽ നിന്ന് Gift അല്ലെങ്കിൽ Will വഴി Capital Asset ലഭിക്കുമ്പോൾ, Previous Owner വാങ്ങിയ Cost of Acquisition തന്നെയാണ് Assessee-യുടെ Cost of Acquisition ആയി എടുക്കേണ്ടത്. ഇതിൽ 1st April 2001-ന് മുമ്പ് വാങ്ങിയ Asset ആണെങ്കിൽ, Fair Market Value അല്ലെങ്കിൽ actual Cost of Acquisition—ഇതിൽ ഏതാണോ കൂടുതൽ, അത് Assessee-ക്ക് COA ആയി substitute ചെയ്യാം."

---

## Enforcement in Code & Pipeline
1. **Autonomous Provision Architect (`pipeline/autonomous_provision_architect.py`)**:
   The prompt template must explicitly instruct Gemini to follow these pronunciation rules for all generated `voice_sync_text`.
2. **Audio Pre-processing / Text Normalizer (`pipeline/synthesize_discrete_points.py`)**:
   Before sending text to Gemini Expressive TTS (`Aoede`), verify that all dates (`dd/mm/yyyy` or `dd-mm-yyyy`) are formatted as `1st April 2001` or clear English so the neural voice never mispronounces them.
