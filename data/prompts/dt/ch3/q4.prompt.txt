  (Clause C: 180 Days Restricted Benefit Rule) (dialogue: "If any asset acquired during the Previous Year is put to use for less than 180 days, Depreciation rate is restricted to 50% of the normal rate for the entire block calculation.")
  (Clause D: Non-Cognizance of Revaluation Value) (dialogue: "The actual consideration or agreed price for transfer of assets during Amalgamation shall be completely ignored while determining Written Down Value under Section 43(6).")
